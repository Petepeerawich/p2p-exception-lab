What the demonstration shows

  1. Investigate. INV-002 bills 108 THB per unit against an order price of 100 THB. Local Qwen3 selects read-only tools to retrieve the invoice, matching results, policy and connected evidence.
  2. Correct. The reviewer applies a fictional corrected invoice at 100 THB per unit and records the evidence reference and reason.
  3. Recheck. Deterministic matching rules validate the updated documents. Saving a correction alone cannot unlock approval.
  4. Decide. The reviewer records a local approval after the checks pass. The case history records the changes. No payment or SAP posting occurs.

Evidence and limitations

The demonstration uses an isolated practice database. The model's proposed action and cited record IDs are validated in code; its narrative still requires human review. The prototype has no SAP connection and does not send messages, post invoices or make payments. Built with AI coding assistance. Fixture tests are not production accuracy measurements.
